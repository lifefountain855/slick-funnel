The market in one sentence

Small businesses don't necessarily need another person to build them a website; they need someone to take ownership of their entire online customer-acquisition system.

That distinction could be your business.

1. Is there actually a pain/need?
Yes—and the pain is surprisingly consistent.

Recent small-business research repeatedly identifies the same problems:

Pain	What the owner actually means
"I need more customers"	Lead generation
"My website sucks"	Poor conversion / credibility
"I don't have time for social media"	Content management
"Nobody finds me on Google"	Local SEO / Google Business
"I get leads but nobody follows up"	Automation
"I don't know if my marketing works"	Analytics
"Marketing is too expensive"	Need affordable, measurable service

One 2025 small-business survey found lead generation was the #1 marketing challenge (29.7%), followed by budget constraints (27.4%). Meanwhile, 55.6% said increasing sales was their primary marketing goal.

Another survey found that 44.6% of small businesses consider marketing overwhelming, while 44% specifically cited consistency as a major challenge.

And a 2025 BrightLocal survey found that cost prevents 36% of SMBs from investing in marketing, while only 40% reported having a dedicated website and 35% having a Google Business Profile.

That's a pretty compelling combination:

Businesses know they need digital marketing → don't have time/expertise → don't want to spend agency-level money → want customers.

That's exactly the gap I'd target.
